Hi! I am a PhD student in Computer Science at George Mason University, advised by Prof. David Porfirio in the Autonomous Robot Interaction (ARI) Lab. I work on human-robot interaction (HRI), with a focus on how people communicate tasks to autonomous robots.

Research

My research focuses on human-robot interaction and end-user robot programming, particularly how users specify tasks for autonomous robots and how they understand and evaluate robot behavior. My work explores:

  • Capturing user intent: helping users express what they truly mean, not just what they literally say, when specifying robot tasks
  • Minimally sufficient programming: specifying just enough for the robot to complete a task while leaving room for its autonomous decision-making
  • Understanding robot behavior: making autonomous robot decisions visible so users can evaluate them and align their expectations
